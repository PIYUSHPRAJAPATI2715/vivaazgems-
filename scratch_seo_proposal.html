1. Executive Summary & Campaign Vision

handicraft.layest.shop specializes in handcrafted, premium wooden pooja mandirs and traditional Indian art pieces. The primary objective of this SEO campaign is to position 5 high-converting, commercial-intent keywords in the Top 3 Google Search Results within 30 days.

Our strategy focuses exclusively on high-value buyers and homeowners living abroad who are searching for permanent, high-quality solid wood temples for their homes.

🎯 High-Intent Audience Strategy: Excluding Renters

Strategic Focus: Renters generally look for small, temporary, or cheap folding temples. To maximize ROI and target actual buyers/homeowners, our content, metadata, and keyword targeting emphasize "Solid Wood", "Custom Handcrafted", "Luxury Home Shrine", and "Permanent Home Mandir". Low-intent terms like "cheap", "rental", "folding", and "plastic mandir" will be strictly excluded.
