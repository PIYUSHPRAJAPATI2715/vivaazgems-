## Office Team User Roles & Access Control

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Security & Workflow Rule**:
> - **Office Staff Logins**: Each team member receives their own user account with the **Shop Manager** role (`products`, `orders`, `pages` — no plugin or theme settings access).
> - **Administrator Access**: Only Harsh and the main designer/developer are Administrators.

## Office Team Handover Training Package

1. **Live Recorded Session**: 1-hour live walkthrough covering daily site management.
2. **10 Short Video Tutorials**:
   - How to add a new calibrated gemstone product.
   - How to add a one-of-a-kind stone with unique carat weight.
   - How to update quantity pieces and tiered pricing.
   - How to change homepage hero block video/banner.
   - How to update footer address or operating hours alone.
   - How to process WooCommerce orders & mark shipped.
   - How to manage WhatsApp inquiry broadcasts.
   - How to upload gem guide blog posts.
   - How to mark products as "Sold" (never delete!).
   - How to edit product SKU codes linked to Instagram Reels.

3. **1-Page Photo Size & Format Cheatsheet**:
   - **Product Photos**: `1200 × 1200 px` square, plain white/cream background, same angle every time.
   - **Hero & Reel Videos**: `9:16` vertical ratio or compressed MP4 (<2 MB).
   - **True Size Diagram**: Made once per shape and size in Canva on a 1 mm grid.
