OUR STORY

Gemstone Sourcing & Craftsmanship from Jaipur Since 1993

[ Big Photo of Jaipur Workshop & Johari Bazar Office ]

Founded in 1993 in Johari Bazar, Jaipur — the gemstone capital of India — Vivaaz Gems & Jewellery specializes in sourcing, cutting, and grading natural loose gemstones, gemstone beads, matched layout suites, and tennis jewelry.

We work directly with ethical mining sources in Sri Lanka, Madagascar, Zambia, and Myanmar, ensuring every gemstone is 100% natural, lab certified, and accurately graded.

How We Work in 5 Steps

1
Source: Direct rough gemstone procurement from ethically verified mines in Ceylon, Tanzania, and Zambia.
2
Cut: Precision lapidary cutting in our Jaipur workshop by master artisans.
3
Grade & Match: Stringent color, clarity, and dimension sorting for calibrated lots and matched suites.
4
Certify: Independent laboratory testing and certification by GIA, IGI, and reputed gem labs.
5
Ship Insured: Secure, insured door-step delivery across India (2-3 days) and worldwide (4-7 days).
